# SEV-1: Friday Deployment

### Your weekend has been escalated.

Friday. 4:59 PM. You're one click away from going home.

Then production goes down. The office lights flicker. Your coworkers start changing.
And Management would really appreciate an ETA.

**SEV-1 is a single-player, third-person hack-and-slash game about surviving
corporate hell with an oversized sword and absolutely no work-life balance.**
Gory action, dark comedy, and an office where “just a quick sync” is a threat.

**The system is down. Management is watching. You're the only engineer online.**

## Make corporate jargon hurt

Chain three-hit melee combos, dodge incoming attacks, and turn office vocabulary
into spectacularly bad news for everything in your way. Your engineer shouts the
move names. Professionalism has left the building.

- **Reply All** — send a cutting crescent through everyone in line.
- **Blame Shift** — dash through enemies, slash them, and make the damage their problem.
- **Circle Back** — pull the room into a fire whirlwind.
- **Works On My Machine** — time your counter and return the complaint to its sender.
- **Force Rollback** — fill your Focus bar, unleash a flurry of blinking sword cuts,
  and finish with a slam.

Sliced monsters, blood, kill streaks, battle grades, and slow-motion finisher cameras
make every resolved ticket a little more personal.

## The office is still open. That's the problem.

Explore **Office After Hours**: deserted desks, Production Operations, a meeting
wing, a haunted pantry, and a failing server corridor leading to the vault.

Talk to the overtime crew. Follow the alarms. Watch an ordinary employee become
something that should never have made it through onboarding. Patrolling monsters
watch for you, call in their colleagues, and turn a quiet investigation into a fight.

The **Blocker** protects its allies. The **Meeting Invite** makes the floor unsafe.
**Scope Creep** gets worse when you ignore it. Even the coffee comes with a policy.
An optional **Quick Sync** offers exactly as much peace as the name suggests.

At the end of the corridor waits **The Variance**.

## Ship it. Live with it.

Take an upgrade now and carry its **Technical Debt** into the next fight. Let
**Burnout** climb and hit harder while becoming easier to hurt. Promise Management
five minutes, give a realistic estimate, or escalate the dependency—and deal with
what follows.

There are voiced conversations, corporate interruptions, original music, and a
blameless postmortem with your name on the action items.

**No programming knowledge required. Knowing office life may make it worse.**

## Acknowledge Incident

The current playtest is **version 0.2.47**, featuring **Incident 001: The Missing
Transaction**. One playable stage, from the Friday interruption to the boss and its
aftermath. Stage One completion saves; **Stage Two is coming later**. Combat,
pacing, and presentation are still being refined with player feedback.

1. Download the **Windows setup installer** or **Intel Mac DMG** from
   [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/acltabontabon/sev-1/releases/latest).
2. On Windows, run the installer. On Mac, open the DMG and drag the app to Applications.
3. Launch the game and choose **Acknowledge Incident**.

No game engine or developer tools needed. The packages are unsigned; see
[installation help](docs/install.md) for requirements and system prompts.
**Keyboard and mouse only.** Contains graphic violence and strong language.

Use **Corporate Policy** for volume, subtitles, camera comfort, and display settings.
**Onboarding Docs** shows the controls in-game.

| WASD / arrows | Move |

| Q / right mouse | Dodge |
| C / middle mouse | Reply All |
| X / attack during a dodge | Blame Shift |
| Z | Circle Back |
| G | Works On My Machine counter |
| V | Force Rollback, when Focus is full |
| E | Talk, interact, or answer the containment call |
| 1 / 2 / 3 | Choose a dialogue response, upgrade, or corporate commitment |
| Tab | Skip supported cinematics; the opening unlocks skipping after first viewing |
| R | Restart the incident |
| Escape | Pause / resume / back |

Chapter completion is saved; mid-incident progress and temporary upgrades are not.
