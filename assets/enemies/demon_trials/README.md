## Modifications and limitations

Godot loads the GLBs and their extracted texture sidecars. Original skin textures
are preserved beneath the existing damage blood and telegraph overlay. Night Demon
also retains its normal map. Legacy Blender materials were converted to PBR; both
models were normalized to 2.1m and the Night Demon Rigify controls were converted
to an exportable hierarchy. The included source scripts were never enabled.

Neither source supplies a complete combat animation set. The six exported clips
are **original, AI-assisted provisional keyframed motions** generated by
[build_demon_trials.py](../../../tools/build_demon_trials.py), not motion capture or
animations supplied by the asset creators. Walk/run foot planting, strike quality,
and the existing bone-collapse waist cuts remain human playtest checkpoints.
The body-only death clip is provisional; normal rendered gameplay uses physical
tumbling or waist cuts with the existing blood effects.
The exporter warned about legacy mesh data and reduced vertices with more than
four bone influences to the strongest four. Both resulting skins were inspected
in the renderer; this is not a claim that every deformation has been accepted.

The source archives remain in the temporary download directory, outside the game.
To rebuild, download the exact URLs, verify the recorded checksums and extract them.
Run Blender 4.5.3 LTS with:

```sh
Blender --background --factory-startup --python tools/build_demon_trials.py -- \
  --night-source /path/to/night/archive \
  --chain-source /path/to/Chained_Demon
```

## Quick review

Run the normal game with `sh tools/godot.sh --path .`: regular Corrupted Record
waves show both body variants. Existing controls and retry behavior apply.
For an isolated side-by-side office review:

```sh
sh tools/godot.sh --path . --script tests/demon_preview.gd
```

Press 1–5 for idle, walk, attack, stagger and death poses; Esc closes the viewer.
`-- --capture` writes `builds/demon_trials.png` and exits. This preview does not
save game progress. It uses actual office lighting at 1920×1080, Forward+.
For rendered strike or physical death evidence, append `-- --capture --pose=Attack`
or `-- --capture --gore`, respectively.

Regression checks: `sh tools/godot.sh --headless --path . --script tests/demon_tests.gd`.
These exercise actual wave spawning, texture preservation, arm motion, damage,
physical slice deaths and reset cleanup. Full checks: `python3 tools/validate.py`.
Neither headless run establishes visual quality or fun.

Verification on 11 October: the full `python3 tools/validate.py` suite passed in
an isolated temporary snapshot of baseline `d0af2a8` plus this session's monster
changes. Isolation avoided incomplete imports from concurrent player/effect work.
Idle, forward claw strike and physical waist cuts were also inspected in the
actual office renderer on Apple M2 Pro, Godot 4.7.2, Metal Forward+, 1920×1080.
No gameplay performance measurement or human fun/animation acceptance is claimed.
