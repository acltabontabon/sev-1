# GARY's snoring (implemented prototype, 11 October 2026)

The sleeping on-call coworker has a soft positional, looping raspy inhale/exhale.
Its close level is -16 dB, held within 1.2 metres of the engineer, fading to silence
at 5 metres. Distance uses the hero rather than the third-person camera; stereo
direction still follows the view. The SFX volume control applies. Hidden bodies,
story/dialogue holds and a dead hero silence it; ordinary tree pause pauses audio.
Walking back or retrying restores it without creating another audio player.
Range and close volume are exported on `game/incidents/sleeper_snoring.gd`.
A small, faint rising `zZ` above his head pulses with the inhalation and fades with
the sound's proximity, identifying the sleeper without a new interaction prompt.

Regression check: `sh tools/godot.sh --headless --path . --script tests/snoring_tests.gd`.
Graphical/audio checkpoint: `sh tools/godot.sh --path . --script tests/snoring_preview.gd -- --listen`.

`assets/audio/sfx/sleeper_snore.wav` is original deterministic oscillator and noise
synthesis generated by [tools/build_snoring.py](../tools/build_snoring.py).
No recordings, outside samples, voice model or real-person imitation are used.
Rights remain with the project's original content; no outside attribution is needed.
The eight-second, mono 22,050 Hz PCM loop contains two varied breaths with silent
ends. Listening and mix acceptance remain with the creator.

Playtest: start Incident 001, approach GARY asleep at his workstation, linger for
two breaths, then walk away and return. Listen for a recognizable snore, a gentle
fade and silence beyond the nearby desks. Check SFX volume, pause, dialogue and R
retry. Report whether the snore is convincing and the volume/range feels right.
