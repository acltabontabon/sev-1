# SEV-1 Game Vision

SEV-1 is a single-player, third-person 3D hack-and-slash action roguelite about an exhausted senior software engineer trying to finish work and go home. Production fails at Friday, 4:59 PM; the office becomes an impossible descent through corporate dysfunction. Death returns the engineer to 4:59 PM. Again.

*The system is down. Management is watching. You're the only engineer online.*

## Player promise

Win stylish, readable fights against the physical consequences of a dysfunctional organization. Learn enemies, commit to attacks, escape danger, and make uncomfortable decisions about short-term relief and later consequences. The action must be enjoyable without its jokes; the jokes must work without programming knowledge.

The audience is action-roguelite players who enjoy expressive combat and dark comedy. Developers get an extra layer of recognition, not exclusive access to the plot or the rules. This is not a technical training simulator.

## Creative pillars

1. **Combat earns the replay.** Responsive movement, weighty attacks, readable threats, deliberate defensive options, and convincing reactions come before content volume or progression systems.
2. **The organization produces the incident.** Conflicting incentives connect the six circles. Individuals can be sympathetic, helpful, or absurd; no job title is inherently the villain.
3. **The visuals take the absurdity seriously.** Believable corporate architecture, cohesive materials, cold office light, incident alarms, and industrial infrastructure establish reality before it breaks. Cinematic effects must preserve combat readability.
4. **Corporate choices have consequences.** Future requests and shortcuts should create understandable tradeoffs, not arbitrary punishment or a morality meter.
5. **A small, finished experience proves the vision.** One excellent encounter and one beautiful office outrank six incomplete environments.

## Decision status

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Established direction | Creative or technical constraint to preserve; does not mean implemented |
| Implemented prototype | Present in the current playable; balance and presentation remain provisional |
| Proposed experiment | Requires a focused prototype and playtest before adoption |
| Deferred | Outside the current increment; no implementation implied |

The identity, Friday loop, six thematic circles, systemic revelation, visual contrast, single-player scope, and Godot 4.7.2/GDScript/Forward+ foundation are established direction. Individual bosses, abilities, progression rules, and the ending are proposals. Six circles are a long-term narrative framework, not a promise to ship six levels before proving the game.

## Current boundary

The playable is a combat laboratory with one opponent and a temporary mannequin. It establishes a foundation, not the finished art style or a narrative episode. Preserve it while refining feel. No new gameplay is authorized by these planning documents alone.

The human creator is the final creative decision-maker and playtester. Codex handles implementation, configuration, and validation in small, reviewable increments.
