# SEV-1 {{VERSION}}: Paperwork, promises, and one very bad birthday

**WORK IN PROGRESS · FRIDAY DEPLOYMENT PLAYTEST**

This build makes corporate nonsense happen **while you play**: your ultimate earns
you a survey, an office birthday becomes an ambush, and the production vault comes
apart around the fight. Between outbreaks, get to know the night shift—the ending

## Your ultimate has a follow-up task

Force Rollback lands. Then People Analytics wants to know how it went.
**Your next hits stamp positive feedback. Ignore the form and the company assumes
consent.** The survey stays out of your controls and disappears during conversations
and cinematics. Please continue fighting while we measure your enthusiasm.

![Force Rollback's mandatory survey beside the current engineer and office](https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/acltabontabon/sev-1/dd23683dbbbac547ce73299d337d98d4e4af1d7d/docs/marketing/v0_2_51_rollback.png)

## Make plans for after work

Personal conversations are easier to find, replies wait for the spoken line, and
the camera gives the crew room to breathe. Ask for something normal. Accept a
coffee or lunch invitation. **Your chosen promise returns in that coworker's
memorial**, alongside the narrated memories and revised walk out of the office.

The pantry's optional birthday is now a voiced party with candles, coworkers,

## Production is now breakable

The expanded vault gives The Variance a larger industrial arena, equipment to
smash, and a crew firefighting entrance. **Your attacks and the boss's can destroy
the props.** Watch the floor, use the dodge lanes, and expect collateral damage.

![The Variance in the expanded production vault, surrounded by industrial equipment](https://media.githubusercontent.com/media/acltabontabon/sev-1/dd23683dbbbac547ce73299d337d98d4e4af1d7d/docs/marketing/v0_2_51_vault.png)

## What to try—and tell us

- Fill Focus and press **V**. Keep fighting after Force Rollback, then try ignoring
  the survey on another use. Did the joke land without getting in your way?
- Choose **“Tell me something normal”** with a coworker and accept an invitation.
  Did that conversation change how the aftermath felt?
- Visit the pantry birthday and finish the vault fight. Were the surprises funny,
  the threats readable, and the pacing right?

**Validation:** full source checks passed locally and on native Windows/Intel Mac
runners. Packaged gameplay checks passed on both; Windows installation/uninstallation
and Mac DMG verification passed. Ordinary GPU/audio playtesting, first-use hitches,
combat balance, comedy and emotional impact still need human feedback.

<summary>First time here? Start and controls</summary>

SEV-1 is a single-player, third-person hack-and-slash through a nighttime office
where coworkers become monsters and Management still wants an ETA.

Choose **Play → The Reconciliation Abyss**. **WASD** moves, **mouse** looks,
**left-click** attacks, **right-click** dodges, **E** interacts, and **Escape** pauses.
Open **Controls** for special moves and **Settings** for audio, display and comfort.

This playtest contains one playable circle: Incident 001, its boss and aftermath.
The later circles are locked. Contains graphic violence and strong language.
