## Make corporate jargon hurt

Chain three-hit combos, dodge incoming attacks, and turn office vocabulary into
spectacularly bad news. Your engineer shouts the move names. Professionalism has
left the building.

- **Reply All** — send a cutting crescent through everyone in line.
- **Blame Shift** — dash through enemies and make the damage their problem.
- **Circle Back** — pull the room into a fire whirlwind.
- **Works On My Machine** — time your counter and return the complaint to its sender.
- **Force Rollback** — fill Focus, unleash blinking sword cuts, and finish with a slam.

## The office is still open. That's the problem.

Explore abandoned desks, Production Operations, a haunted pantry, and a meeting
room that should have been an email. Talk to the overtime crew. Trigger outbreaks.
Watch ordinary employees become monsters, then follow the failing server corridor
to **The Variance**.

Pick a free temporary power-up. Burnout makes you dangerous and vulnerable.
Your promise to Management has consequences. There are voiced conversations,
original music, bloody finishers, and a blameless postmortem with your name on it.

This playtest adds a mandatory satisfaction survey after Force Rollback—your next
hits count as positive feedback. Survive Kevin's pantry birthday, smash equipment
in the expanded vault, and spend a moment with the crew before the aftermath.
The company remembers your productivity. The ending remembers your promises.

**The system is down. Management is watching. You're the only engineer online.**

## Your first shift

Launch the game and choose **Play** → **The Reconciliation Abyss**.
**WASD** moves, **mouse** controls the camera, **left-click** attacks,
**right-click** dodges, **E** interacts, and **Escape** pauses.
**Controls** lists every special move; **Settings** holds audio, display and comfort options.

This playtest includes **Incident 001: The Missing Transaction**—one playable
stage through its boss and aftermath. Stage One completion saves; replay preserves
it. Stage Two is coming later. Contains graphic violence and strong language.

Combat balance, pacing, voices, music and performance are still being refined.
Automated checks cover game logic and package launch; ordinary graphical and audio
playtesting on Windows and Intel Macs is still needed.
